### M4.6 / M4.9: crossing and pinched common-support domains

Revision-2 §5.2 requires actual manifold union/difference, uncapped exact sheets,
no overlapping area or T-junctions, and unique provenance for reference remapping.
Previously, `combine_surfaces` reached the globally ordered interval path and
rejected boundaries that exchanged order. The replacement partitions each exact
rational generating curve at all affine station-boundary roots, arranges the
resulting domains, reconstructs exact rational trims, splits ruling edges at every
incident vertex, welds seams, and validates edge incidence and vertex links.
Bernstein derivative-root isolation retains even-multiplicity contacts. Interval
order is certified by complete curve extrema, not by a midpoint classification.

The real cubic is `C(t)=(6t,9t-12t²+3t³)`, with source controls
`(0,0),(2,3),(4,2),(6,0)`. Tests retain these exact cases:

- Ordered domain: target `[0,3+0.1x]`, tool `[1,4+0.2x]`; Add area
  `integral |C'|*(4+0.2x) dt`, subsequent Remove area equal to cubic length L.
- Crossing: target `[0,3+0.1x]`, tool `[1,3.6-0.1x]`; Add uses the true
  piecewise maximum with switch at x=3. Its area, topology, mesh, provenance and
  subsequent operation are checked; no global-order fallback is accepted.
- Valid endpoint pinch: Remove leaves `[0,0.1y]`. It is a disk with two exact
  curved boundary edges and no artificial zero-length ruling. It has positive
  area, valid incidence and downstream tessellation.
- Invalid interior pinch: Remove leaves a domain with a zero-width interior
  tangent at the cubic's maximum y. Vertex-link validation deterministically
  rejects `nonmanifold pinched sheet vertex`, rather than constructing two sheets
  joined at one nonmanifold point.

Area oracles integrate the explicit polynomial speed independently of production
surface-Jacobian integration. The original 16,384-interval Simpson bound is retained
(error below 1e-9 mm² for the stated weighted integrand). Opposite direction,
translated source, knot refinement and degree elevation remain covered.

`curved_crossing_and_pinched_scopes_use_real_preparation_identity_and_atomic_rejection`

### M4.7: partial nonparallel curved ThroughAll

Revision-2 §6.1 requires the furthest actual positive intersection of the complete
swept section, independently of merge scope, plus `max(10*eps,1e-6mm)`. The old
query proved only full containment. The new query clips exact positive-weight
Bezier control hulls to the projected source hull to obtain conservative scalar
upper bounds, subdivides exact curves/ruled patches, and uses actual feasible
curve/ruling intersections as lower witnesses. It terminates only when that gap
is at most 1e-9 mm. Bounds are never the final hit or model geometry. Cancellation
and explicit complexity/depth failures prevent an uncertified success. Filled
footprints also use exact trim coverage, including holes; open chains are queried
as curves. Parallel projected rulings have analytic root witnesses, so isolated
coincidences do not depend on a sample grid.

The nonparallel patch has lower controls `(0,0,4),(2,3,7),(4,2,6),(6,0,4)`

`combine_partitioned_sweeps` → `equivalent_sweep_support`. The latter proves only

Pinned API investigation (all production pins remain **4.2.2**):

- `brepkit-algo::gfa::{boolean,boolean_with_tolerance}` and operations split/section
  take `SolidId`, not open face sets. Capping/thickening would change the problem.
- `brepkit-math::nurbs::bezier_clip::curve_curve_intersect_full` exposes overlap

- Surface intersection uses seeded marching and fitted curves; line/surface
  intersection uses a sample grid/Newton refinement. These cannot prove complete
  exact arbitrary curved coincidence, trim arrangement or finite-hit absence.
