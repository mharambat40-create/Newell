"Selection-box scrollbar states — README_UI.md §21"
Track strings are top-to-bottom; quoted spaces are significant.
Every state has ▲ above its three track cells and ▼ below (five rows total).
N=0: blank list, no scrollbar; N=1–5: all items, no scrollbar.
N=6:  ["┃┃╹", "╻┃┃"]
N=7:  ["┃┃ ", "╻┃╹", " ┃┃"]
N=8:  ["┃╹ ", "╻┃ ", " ┃╹", " ╻┃"]
N=9:  ["┃  ", "╻╹ ", " ┃ ", " ╻╹", "  ┃"]
N=10: ["╹  ", "╻  ", " ╹ ", " ╻ ", "  ╹", "  ╻"]
N>10: one occupied half-cell, p=round_half_up(5*s/(N-5)).
These states apply only to selection-box content lists.
